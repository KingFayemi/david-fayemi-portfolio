Case study

LemFi Credit

16K+users onboarded in first year of launch

Company
LemFi
Year
2024
Role
Senior Product Designer, Credit
Discipline
Product Design

LemFi's credit product suite serving users across the UK — from the core credit card to Send Now, Pay Later — built for immigrants the financial system was never designed for.

Context

LemFi is a YC-backed fintech building the go-to financial app for immigrants. When I joined the credit team, the company was expanding beyond remittances into lending products that could meet real spending needs for diaspora users in the UK.

Credit is high-stakes: trust, clarity, and compliance have to show up in every screen. The suite needed to feel like LemFi — warm, direct, mobile-first — while carrying the weight of regulated financial products.

Role

I founded and lead design across the credit product suite. That spans discovery and UX for the core credit card, Send Now Pay Later, onboarding flows, and the design-system patterns that keep credit experiences consistent with the rest of the app.

I work closely with product, engineering, compliance, and brand — translating regulatory constraints into flows people actually understand on a phone.

Problem

Immigrant users are often underserved by traditional credit: thin files, unfamiliar product language, and apps that assume a UK-native mental model. We needed to explain eligibility, limits, and repayment without friction — and without hiding the terms that matter.

Process

We started with research on how LemFi users already move money — when they borrow, what they compare against, and where they drop off. I mapped those journeys into a shared credit IA so card and BNPL could reuse components instead of diverging.

Prototypes went through compliance review early. We iterated on disclosure patterns, error states, and empty states until support tickets and funnel data showed users could self-serve through activation.

Outcome

The credit suite launched to LemFi users across the UK. In the first year, more than 16,000 users onboarded — validating that clear, immigrant-first credit design could scale inside a remittance-native product.

Credit patterns now feed back into LemFi's broader design system, so the next products ship faster without losing the tone that makes LemFi feel human.
