HI, I'M DAVID FAYEMI

I'm a senior product designer who builds financial products for people the system was never built for. I care about making them feel clear, trustworthy, and accessible to communities mainstream finance overlooks.

Fintech is where I've spent most of my career: remittance, credit, lending, and banking for markets where trust and clarity matter most.

At LemFi I lead design for the credit suite and our next-gen design system, including extending our tooling into AI workflows so teams ship on-brand work faster.

I also host The BluePrint Series, published Road Guide to Design Systems, and mentor designers through communities like Roadtrip and HNG.
